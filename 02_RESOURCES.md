# TADA! Resources

Tactile world-building is an ongoing adventure. During and beyond this TADA! map, we invite exploration of various other tactile adventures, such as:

- [BlindSVG.com — Marco Salsiccia's guide to nonvisual computer design using SVG](https://blindsvg.com)
- [Marconius.com/svg — Marco Salsiccia's personal SVG projects and downloadable code](https://marconius.com/svg)
- [BTactile — tactile files repository](https://btactile.com/)
- [Dimensions Lab — Andrew Heiskell Braille and Talking Book Library (NYPL)](https://www.nypl.org/about/locations/heiskell/dimensions)
- [Minh Ha — tactile coloring books and tiles](https://www.linkedin.com/in/minhha93/)
- [Ka Li — National Network for Equitable Library Service (NNELS)](https://disabilitybandwidth.com/s2-episode-5-ka-li/)
- [MAD Lab — Lighthouse for the Blind and Visually Impaired, San Francisco](https://lighthouse-sf.org/design/)
- [Makers Making Change — Neil Squire](https://www.makersmakingchange.com/s/)
- Neal McKenzie's tactile teaching aides, math a11y, and lessons
- [PIAF Teaching Aids](https://sites.google.com/scoe.org/scoevs/vi-resources#h.wk8u946epchq)
- [Mountain Lakes Public Library Makerspace — Ian Coleman](https://m.facebook.com/LakerMakersClub)
- [Sensational Books — Ann Cunningham](https://sensationalbooks.com/)
- [See3D — Caroline Karbowski](https://see3d.org/)
- [Abigale Stangl, Ph.D — Georgia Institute of Technology](https://abigalestangl.com/)
- [Advanced Activities Section C: 3D Printable STEM Resources](embeds/resources_page/3D_PRINTABLE_STEM_RESOURCES.md)
